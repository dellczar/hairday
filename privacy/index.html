Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your bookings: your name, email, the bookings you make and, if you allow it, your phone’s location (it stays on your phone).
  • Your card goes to Stripe, our payment company. Hairday never sees or stores your full card number.
  • We don’t sell your information, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time (Profile → Delete account), or email hello@hairday.app to see or delete your information.

This policy explains how HairDay, Inc. (“Hairday”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Hairday app for iPhone and Android and the website hairday.app (together, the “Service”). Hairday connects customers with salons, barbers, braiders and other beauty professionals (“salons”) so customers can book and pay for appointments.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account

  • Name and email address. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address that service shares with us. If you use Sign in with Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we receive a private relay address instead.
  • Phone number, if you choose to add one.
  • We don’t use passwords. Sign-in happens through Apple, Google or a one-time code sent to your email.

When you book

  • The salon, services, professional, date and time you choose, any tip, and the booking’s status (for example confirmed, cancelled or completed).
  • Payment details are entered into Stripe’s secure payment form and stored by Stripe. We receive only limited details from Stripe, such as the card brand, the last four digits and whether a payment succeeded.

What you choose to share

  • Reviews and ratings you write. Reviews are public and show your first name and the first letter of your last name (for example “Dana T.”).
  • Favorites you save, and messages you send us.

From your phone, only if you allow it

  • Location. When you open Explore, the app can ask to use your location to sort salons by distance. Your location is used on your phone to measure distances and is not sent to or stored on our servers. If you say no, we measure from a point in New York instead.
  • Notifications. If you turn on notifications, we store a notification token for your phone so we can send booking confirmations and reminders.
  • Photos and camera (salon owners), to add photos of the business and team.
  • Contacts (salon owners), only when the owner taps “Import from contacts”, and only for the people they pick.
  • Calendar, only when you tap “Add to calendar”. The event is added on your phone; we don’t read your calendar.

If you run a business on Hairday

  • Business details you add: name, address, hours, services, prices, photos, team members and policies. These are shown publicly on your salon page.
  • Your client list, including names, emails and phone numbers you enter or import, and visit history.
  • To pay salons, Stripe collects identity, tax and bank details directly during Stripe’s own onboarding. Hairday doesn’t see or store bank account numbers or Social Security numbers. We receive your Stripe account status (for example whether payouts are enabled).

Collected automatically

  • Basic technical information needed to run the Service, such as your device type, operating system, app version and server logs (which include IP addresses).
  • We don’t use advertising identifiers, ad networks or third-party analytics trackers.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To make, change, cancel and charge bookings, including the cancellation and no-show rules shown when you book.
  • To send booking emails and notifications, for example confirmations, reminders, cancellations and review requests. We don’t send marketing messages without your permission.
  • To show salons, prices, open times and reviews, and to help salon owners run their day.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, keep the Service safe, fix problems and improve it.
  • To follow the law, for example tax and accounting rules, and to enforce our Terms.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only as described here. We don’t sell your personal information and we don’t share it for targeted advertising.

  • The salon you book with sees your name, the booking details and the contact details on your account, so they can serve you. Salons are responsible for how they use that information in their own business.
  • The public sees reviews (with your first name and last initial) and salon pages.
  • Service providers who run parts of the Service for us and may only use the information to do that:
    ProviderWhat they do
    SupabaseDatabase, sign-in, photo storage and server functions
    StripeCard payments and salon payouts (Stripe’s privacy policy)
    ResendSending sign-in codes and booking emails
    Expo, Apple and GoogleDelivering phone notifications
    Apple and GoogleSign in with Apple, Google sign-in, and maps (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android)
  • For legal reasons, if we must by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or Hairday.
  • If the business changes hands, for example in a merger or sale, information may move to the new owner, who must keep following this policy.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example payment and tax records, which are usually kept for up to seven years) and information needed to resolve disputes or prevent fraud. Reviews you wrote are deleted with your account.

5. Your choices and rights

  • See, correct or download your information: change your name and details in the app under Profile, or email us.
  • Turn off location, notifications, photos or contacts at any time in your phone’s Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account, or by email (see below).

Depending on where you live, for example California and other U.S. states with privacy laws, you may have extra rights, such as asking what we hold about you, asking us to delete or correct it, and opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Hairday doesn’t sell or share personal information in that way. To use any of these rights, email hello@hairday.app. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we won’t treat you differently for asking.

6. Deleting your account

In the app: open Profile, tap Delete account at the bottom, then Yes, delete my account. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email: if you can’t open the app, email hello@hairday.app from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my Hairday account”. We’ll confirm and complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted: your profile, favorites, reviews, saved cards (removed from Stripe), notification settings and photos. If you own a salon on Hairday, its listing, services, team, photos and booking history are deleted too.

What is kept: records of past bookings and payments at other salons stay without your name, because salons and tax rules need them (see section 4).

Before you delete: cancel any upcoming bookings (yours, or at your salon) first. If a recent visit hasn’t been charged yet, you can delete your account once that payment has gone through.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit, access rules in our database so people can see only their own information, and secure storage on your phone for your sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

8. Children

Hairday is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. You must be at least 18, or have a parent or guardian’s permission, to book. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we’ll delete it.

9. Where information is stored

Hairday is run from the United States, and information is stored and processed in the United States.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If the changes are important, we’ll tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
